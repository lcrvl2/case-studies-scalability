# BREVO: OPENING SPAIN WITHOUT BUYING TEN EXTRA LICENCES

**Will, Sales Director**

---

## A MARKET THEY HAD ALREADY TRIED

Brevo is a customer platform that helps clients create meaningful relationships across all parts
of their marketing system. Will has been there eight years, and over that period his main
responsibility has been opening new markets, Spain and the UK among them.

They had already gone at Spain before Scalability came in. He is precise about what was missing,
and only one of the three gaps is about expertise.

> "Before we started working with Scalability we did try to attack Spanish market and others. And
> the main issues we found are that we lacked expertise in those markets, but also we lacked the
> tools and the time to dedicate to set everything up well."

## THE TOOLING, AND THE LICENCES THEY DIDN'T BUY

Scalability took the technical setup off their hands. Because they already had the stack, and
because they sat in Brevo's Slack, the feedback came back fast and the tooling question solved
itself.

> "They're able to really say, okay, this is working, this isn't, this is the tools we need, and
> we didn't have to take 10 extra licenses just to test in the Spanish market."

Testing a market normally means buying the tools before knowing whether the market answers. Here
that cost stayed off the table.

> "Working with Scalability is really excellent because it feels like you have a partner inside
> the company that's very data driven. So every two weeks or a month, we'll do a business review
> with them and get really precise data of what's working, what isn't, how we're changing the
> messaging. This allows us to be confident when we're attacking a new market."

## THREE PILLARS, IN HIS OWN ORDER

Will lists three results, and signing clients comes third.

The first is the market itself. Spain had been the previous year's big challenge, and it was the
first market Brevo opened through outbound.

> "We managed to successfully open a market that we haven't done in outbound before for Spain, and
> this was a really big challenge for us last year."

The second is what carries over. What they tested in Spain now shapes how they look at the next
territories, even knowing each one differs.

> "What we tested in the Spanish market is really influencing how we're looking at The UK, The
> Nordics, The US, even if we know each market is different."

And the third.

> "And the third one, and it's obviously the most important, is we've managed to sign clients."

## RUNNING ALONGSIDE THEIR OWN GROWTH OPS TEAM

Brevo already had a growth ops team running similar strategies on other markets. Scalability
worked next to it rather than replacing it, which gave Will something he could not have set up on
his own.

> "They were able to come on board, work with our growth ops team, who are already doing very
> similar strategies on different markets, but we can almost AB test and have this kind of
> external internal comparison, and it was very valuable for us."

Two teams running comparable strategies on different markets, inside the same company. That is a
benchmark an agency alone cannot provide, and an internal team alone has no one to measure against.

> "If you're a company that's trying to grow internationally, increase your pipeline, even on your
> home markets, Scalability did that for us. I would say that if anyone's looking, Scalability is
> a great partner to work with."
