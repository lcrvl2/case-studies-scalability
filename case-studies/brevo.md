# BREVO : OPENING SPAIN WITHOUT BUYING TEN EXTRA LICENCES

**Will, Sales Director**

---

## A MARKET THEY HAD ALREADY TRIED TO CRACK

Brevo is a customer platform that helps clients build meaningful relationships across their
marketing systems. Will has been with the company for eight years, and his main responsibility
over that period has been opening new markets, Spain and the UK among them.

Spain had already been attempted before Scalability came in. The attempt had not worked, and Will
is precise about why.

> « Before we started working with Scalability we did try to attack Spanish market and others. And
> the main issues we found are that we lacked expertise in those markets, but also we lacked the
> tools and the time to dedicate to set everything up well. »

Three gaps, then, and only one of them is about skill. The other two are about capacity: the
tooling, and the time to configure it properly.

## THE LICENCE PROBLEM NOBODY BUDGETS FOR

Testing a new market with an in-house team carries a cost that rarely appears in the business
case. You have to buy the tools before you know whether the market responds.

Because Scalability already had the stack and sat in Brevo's Slack, that cost disappeared.

> « They're able to really say, okay, this is working, this isn't, this is the tools we need, and
> we didn't have to take 10 extra licenses just to test in the Spanish market. »

The same proximity produces the feedback loop Will values most.

> « Working with Scalability is really excellent because it feels like you have a partner inside
> the company that's very data driven. So every two weeks or a month, we'll do a business review
> with them and get really precise data of what's working, what isn't, how we're changing the
> messaging. This allows us to be confident when we're attacking a new market. »

## THREE PILLARS, IN HIS OWN ORDER

Will structures the outcome himself, and the order he chooses is worth noting. Signing clients
comes third.

**First, the market opened.** Spain had been the previous year's big challenge, and it was the
first market Brevo opened through outbound.

> « We managed to successfully open a market that we haven't done in outbound before for Spain,
> and this was a really big challenge for us last year. »

**Second, what they learned carries over.** What worked in Spain now shapes how Brevo looks at
other territories, even knowing each market differs.

> « What we tested in the Spanish market is really influencing how we're looking at The UK, The
> Nordics, The US, even if we know each market is different. »

**Third, the clients.**

> « And the third one, and it's obviously the most important, is we've managed to sign clients. »

## AN INTERNAL COMPARISON THEY COULD NOT RUN ALONE

Brevo has a growth ops team already running similar strategies on other markets. Rather than
replacing it, Scalability worked alongside it, and that produced something Will did not expect.

> « They were able to come on board, work with our growth ops team, who are already doing very
> similar strategies on different markets, but we can almost AB test and have this kind of
> external internal comparison, and it was very valuable for us. »

Two teams, two methods, the same company. The comparison runs continuously.

> « If you're a company that's trying to grow internationally, increase your pipeline, even on
> your home markets, Scalability did that for us. I would say that if anyone's looking,
> Scalability is a great partner to work with. »
